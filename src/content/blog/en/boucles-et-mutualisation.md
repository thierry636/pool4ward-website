A full outbound leg. An empty return. “We should find a return flow.”

On the map, the routes cross. But the schedules don't match. Volumes fluctuate. The vehicle isn't suitable. And the ideal flow disappears for three months.

**Finding two complementary trips isn't enough to build a loop that holds all year round.**

The real difficulty is recurrence: getting days, volumes, equipment and delivery constraints to line up, without detours and waiting time eating up the gains.

A single site doesn't always have the flows it needs. Several subsidiaries or several shippers can, together, build a more regular demand.

**A loop can stay recurring without being identical every week.**

With dynamic milk runs, collections and deliveries are combined and adjusted as needs change, within a framework agreed with the carrier.

That makes it possible to build a year-round purchasing recurrence from flows that, individually, don't offer one.

At Pool4ward, we help to:

- Identify recurrences and complementarities between flows.
- Build loops between sites, subsidiaries and shippers.
- Simulate costs, empty kilometres and operational feasibility.
- Define commitments, adjustment rules and gain sharing with carriers.

**The goal: an organisation that cuts empty kilometres and gives the carrier visibility, week after week.**

A loop that works once is an opportunity. A loop you can adapt all year round is a transport plan.
