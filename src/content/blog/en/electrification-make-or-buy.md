No need to hire Oompa-Loompas for your delivery rounds. But electric deserves to reopen a question often settled years ago: **which building blocks of your transport are you better off controlling yourself?**

“We outsource because it isn't our business.” The reasoning still holds. The economics, though, are changing.

An electric truck runs on a charger, connected power capacity, charging windows and an energy contract. **And some of those resources may already be on your premises.**

Your land, your grid connection and your operating hours can change the economics of the different set-ups.

Owning your truck and employing your driver then deserves a look. So do the in-between solutions: rental with driver, a dedicated truck run by a carrier, charging on your site…

**Who owns the truck and who operates it don't have to be the same company.**

Four building blocks need to be compared:

- Who provides the energy and the charging?
- Who finances the vehicle?
- Who organises operations and drivers?
- Who absorbs the volume peaks and troughs?

On that last point, other shippers can make the difference. Pooling flows and commitments can improve vehicle utilisation and make investment easier.

At Pool4ward, we reopen this trade-off on your real flows: full costs, operational constraints, risks and collaboration opportunities.

**Before ordering the electric truck, let's look at what deserves to come back home.**
