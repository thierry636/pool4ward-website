Securing capacity. Covering a new corridor. Finding suitable equipment. Diversifying partners.

Each objective calls for the right carriers. The challenge is finding them among tens of thousands of players in Europe.

We often start with the names we know. Then recommendations, searches, chasers… with the risk of missing relevant partners.

**The right carrier for one lane isn't necessarily the right one for the next.**

Corridors, equipment, frequencies and operational constraints change the selection. An effective consultation therefore starts before the specifications are sent out.
